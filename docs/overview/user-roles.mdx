# User roles

> Permissions and responsibilities for players, challengers, room participants, market makers, and builders.

## Role summary

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Player | Connects a wallet, picks a market, and takes a side |
| Challenger | Opens a duel and shares the accept link |
| Opponent | Accepts a duel before its deadline |
| Room participant | Joins Up or Down in a shared pot |
| Market maker | Posts quotes to the DreamDEX book |
| Builder | Deploys escrows, configures networks, and verifies protocol assumptions |

## Player

Players interact through the console UI. They can browse live markets, inspect the current strike and status, and choose one of the three modes.

| Capability | Surface |
| --- | --- |
| Connect wallet | Privy |
| View live markets | Console market list |
| Take a side | Run, room, or duel |
| Claim winning outcome | Result screen |

## Challenger and opponent

A duel has exactly two parties and equal stakes.

| Party | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Challenger | Chooses market, side, stake, and accept deadline |
| Opponent | Accepts before the deadline and stakes the same amount |
| Winner | Redeems the full pot after market resolution |
| Loser | Holds a losing leg that redeems for zero without reverting |

## Room participant

Room participants can join either side with any stake. A room is parimutuel: the winning side splits the whole pot by contribution.

## Builder

Builders must verify live venue assumptions before deploying. Trade Rush includes `doctor`, `probe`, and e2e scripts because several protocol details differ from the original brief.
